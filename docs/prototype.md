## Better Brand Names

Instead of **DocsNep**, consider a video-focused brand:

* ClipNep AI
* VideoNep AI
* InfoVideo AI
* Chhotelal AI
* StoryShorts AI
* FactVideo AI
* NepShorts AI
* VidScript AI

For your goal of creating **short and long informational videos automatically**, **VidScript AI** or **FactVideo AI** would be stronger SaaS brands than DocsNep because they immediately communicate the product's purpose.
